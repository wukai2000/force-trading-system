## What conflicts (must trade off)

These pairs cannot be maximized together.

1. **Hunt vs freeze.** A new domain census, Force 4 lock, FS-0001.v2, or
   Item-106 stage-2 fights Path A and the one-frozen rule. Pick A or hunt.
   Not both.
2. **Identification vs retune.** Improving the 2014 predicate after seeing
   edge=0.025 is Option-B on an identity. Path B forbids it. A new intensity
   form is a new version after seal, not a quiet edit.
3. **Auditor purity vs book ambition.** Path C’s auditor-only product never
   needs transmission. The live goal *does* need a later transmission step.
   Do not require transmission this quarter. Do not delete it from the
   definition of done-someday.
4. **Official clocks vs market leftover.** Measurement-architecture search
   and “beat SPY residual” are different sports. Q4 already chose clocks
   first. Do not score E1.I with an IR. Do not call a QUALIFIES census row
   a Force.
5. **D now vs B now.** Refusing uniqueness before vintage `I_t` exists is a
   different claim than “this identity failed as-revised smoke.”
6. **Two frozen slots.** FS-0001.v1 occupies the slot. MTS-0001 is a
   measurement freeze, not a second Force. Do not promote MTS-0001 into a
   Force id.
