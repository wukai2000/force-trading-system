## Would be a breakthrough (does not exist in official architecture)

1. A **territorial freight expenditure** series: monetary operating cost of freight movements *on national territory*, freight-only, mode-aligned to official tkm, annual.
2. An official **freight input-cost deflator** whose basket is labour + fuel + vehicle user-cost + purchased services, not an output SPPI and not GDP.
3. IEA EEI **open** country×mode energy so Observable A (tkm/energy) can be coverage-wired the way tkm already is — still not Observable 2, still not T5.

Until (1) and (2) exist as official methodology, not as an analyst splice, `resource_unit_cost` stays not constructible.
